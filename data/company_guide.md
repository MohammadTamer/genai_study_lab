# GenAI StudyLab Company Guide

## Annual Leave
Employees receive 21 annual leave days per calendar year. New employees who join after July 1 receive a prorated entitlement.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Customer-facing teams may have additional on-site requirements.

## Training Budget
Each employee can request up to $1,500 per year for approved professional-development courses, certifications, or conferences.

## AI Usage
Employees may use approved generative AI tools for drafting, summarization, coding assistance, and research support. Confidential customer information and private credentials must not be pasted into external AI systems.

## Security
API keys must be stored in environment variables or an approved secret manager. Never commit API keys to source control.
